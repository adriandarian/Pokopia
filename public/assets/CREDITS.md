## Decor models (public/assets/decor/*.glb)

All CC0 1.0 (public domain), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl). Licence text shipped inside each pack (License.txt) and verified on the pack page.
Models were flattened to a single mesh and the shared colormap texture was baked into vertex colours (no texture) with a small node script (@gltf-transform/core); nothing else was changed.

- Fantasy Town Kit 2.0 - https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit - walls, roofs, chimney, planks, fence, hedges, lantern, windmill blades, stall stool/table, bench, pillars, round tree, rocks, fountain, road tile.
- Furniture Kit 2.0 - https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit - bear (plushies), bench, tableRound, rugSquare.
- Survival Kit 2.0 - https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit - signpost, rock-flat.
